What does a scalar PRS hide?

SANGHA Research Systems · Thomas Gross, PhD

A reproducible public-reference workflow maps signed Alzheimer genetic effects through declared variant-to-gene mappings and GTEx tissue/context expression to individualized biological allocation profiles. The objective is not to replace scalar PRS, but to measure what it compresses away and which higher-level structure survives reasonable representation changes.

Representation robustness

Promote only what survives the analyst’s choices.

Construct

Signed allocation profiles

Map signed GWAS effects through declared gene and tissue/context representations into individual allocation profiles.

Decompose

Make cancellation visible

Separate centered risk-positive and protective contributions rather than silently absorbing them into a net score.

Audit compression

Retain residual structure

Measure how much higher-dimensional allocation is explained by scalar PRS and preserve what remains unresolved.

Challenge

Replace plausible annotations

Freeze an alternative biological vocabulary and test whether the higher-level structure persists.

Refuse

Deny hostile inputs promotion

Use weak or permissive coordinate constructions as controls and assign them no positive mechanistic weight when they fail.

Validate

Require held-out value

Phenotype claims remain closed until retained features improve a prespecified held-out or external decision.

Inference boundary

This is a structure result, not a phenotype result.

No Alzheimer phenotype, treatment response, donor-tissue archetype, or causal vascular result is established by this analysis. The stable grouping is a representation-robust descriptive result. Incremental phenotype value remains open and requires held-out or external validation.

Open question

When does allocation become a quantitative phenotype?

Which retained allocation features add reproducible information beyond scalar PRS under held-out phenotype prediction, donor stratification, or mechanistically targeted experimental design?

That question connects directly to the RBD → PD EEG work: in both cases, the scientific object of interest is not the most convenient scalar summary, but the latent structure that remains stable enough to deserve promotion.
