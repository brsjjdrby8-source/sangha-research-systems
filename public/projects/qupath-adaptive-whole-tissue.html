Adaptive Whole-Tissue Measurement for QuPath

SANGHA Research Systems × Cherie Stringer, PhD

A reproducible exact/adaptive whole-tissue measurement system for QuPath pixel classifiers, developed from a production pathology workflow and generalized into a parallel, resumable measurement architecture with statistically controlled early termination.

Reference

Whole-tissue exact measurement

The validated reference workflow exhaustively partitions annotated tissue, measures classifier-positive and classifier-negative area, and pools those measurements into a whole-tissue result.

Systems

Parallel, resumable execution

QuPath remains the measurement kernel. Nextflow owns image-level dispatch, isolation, retry and provenance; Make exposes simple human-facing verbs for exact measurement, estimation, verification and resume.

Inference

Adaptive stopping, after parity

The adaptive path will use spatially balanced sampling with a finite-population ratio estimator and LLN/CLT-derived precision criteria. It remains disabled until exact numerical parity is demonstrated.
